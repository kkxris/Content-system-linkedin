# content-pillars

Every post should serve at least one pillar. Ideally two — that's when things go viral. If a draft doesn't fit any pillar, question whether to post it.

**NEW: the mix below is tracked automatically.** See `pillar-mix-tracker` (once set up), which tallies the last 10 logged posts against these targets and flags drift.

## 1. AI (40% of posts)

Practical, hands-on AI content — tools, applications, and developments your audience can actually use. NOT generic "AI is changing everything."

Sub-angles:

- **Tactical wins**: "3 prompts every marketer should save right now"
- **Debunking fear**: "I thought Claude Code would delete my files. Here's what actually happens."
- **Case studies from students**: "Anna, a PM, now runs her weekly report in 10 minutes"
- **Emerging developments**: new tools/releases worth knowing, explained for non-technical readers
- **Lived-experience POV**: "Everyone's teaching prompt engineering wrong" — only when it comes from something she's seen or taught, never a generic hot take

## 2. Marketing (25% of posts)

Marketing strategy and growth, told through what she's actually built and tested — not textbook advice.

Sub-angles:

- Content systems and positioning (how Co-Work & markets itself)
- Growth tactics that worked (and ones that didn't)
- Lessons for founders/businesses marketing without a team
- Framework IP: named frameworks she's developed ("The 4 stages of...")

## 3. Founder's Journey (20% of posts)

The reality of building Co-Work & / SocialFlo — not a highlight reel. This is what makes her a person, not a brand.

Sub-angles:

- Decisions, experiments, and failures along the way
- Leaving corporate / going independent
- Immigrant / outsider identity in Berlin (top performer historically)
- Milestones and what they actually took

## 4. Berlin Community & Workshops (15% of posts)

The reason people come to Vidushi and not a YouTuber. In-person, local, real people in a room.

Sub-angles:

- Workshop stories (who came, what happened, what surprised her)
- Behind-the-scenes (setting up, prepping, day-of energy)
- Berlin-specific asks ("Berlin peeps, need a venue for 30")
- Participant testimonials and session insights, with names + faces

## The mix — every 10 posts should be roughly:

- 4 × Pillar 1 (AI)
- 2-3 × Pillar 2 (Marketing)
- 2 × Pillar 3 (Founder's Journey)
- 1-2 × Pillar 4 (Berlin Community & Workshops)

**Workshop-mention rule: max 1 in every 3 posts.** Otherwise audience burns out.

---

## Tagging rule

Every entry in the post log and every draft must carry a `pillar:` tag (1-4, comma-separated if it serves two). This is what feeds the pillar-mix tracker — untagged posts can't be counted, so the mix tracking silently breaks if this is skipped.
