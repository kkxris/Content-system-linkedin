# posting-rhythm

*Pillars decide the topic. This file decides everything else about how a post goes out.*

## 1. Publishing cadence (provisional — needs validation)

**Starting cadence: 3 posts/week.** This is a provisional baseline, not derived from existing performance data — we don't yet have cadence-vs-engagement history to base it on. Validate it once post-log has 6-8 weeks of data: compare engagement on weeks with 2 vs. 3 vs. 4 posts before locking this in.

- **Workshop launch weeks**: up to 4 posts (announcement, value-led, social proof, reminder — see sequencing below)
- **Quiet weeks**: as low as 2, never zero — consistency matters more than volume
- **Major AI/industry news weeks**: one slot can flex to a timely trend-response post, bumping (not replacing) the week's plan

Quality over frequency: a missed week does less damage than three generic posts in a row. If there's nothing worth saying, don't manufacture cadence.

## 2. Format taxonomy (separate from pillars — any pillar can use any format)

| Format | Purpose | When to use | Priority (based on existing data) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Personal story / confession** | Vulnerability, relatability | Founder's Journey primarily, any pillar at its emotional core | **Highest** — this is what's landed >150 likes historically (racism story, Claude-update joy, workshop full-house) |
| **Community question / ask** | Engagement, warm-lead capture | Workshop/warm-lead posts | **High** — "Berlin network, I need your help" hit 163 likes; pairs with Signature move #1 ("Comment [WORD]") |
| **Educational breakdown / numbered list** | Shareable resource | AI or Marketing pillar | **Untested gap, high priority to test** — zero numbered-list posts in the last 20; this is the queued experiment from voice.md |
| **Contrarian take / opinion** | Re-engage people who think they're advanced | AI or Marketing, only from lived experience | **Test, not default** — "Stop X" pattern is queued but unrun |
| **Practical tip / tool / experiment** | Demonstrate usefulness | AI pillar mainly | Medium — works best framed as a moment ("I asked Claude to do X"), not a dry tip list |
| **Behind-the-scenes / build-in-public** | Warmth, authenticity | Berlin Community & Workshops, Founder's Journey | Medium — pairs naturally with PS-ending signature move |
| **Workshop / event announcement** | Direct promotion | Berlin Community & Workshops only | **Capped** — see sequencing rules below |
| **Social proof / participant outcome / recap** | Credibility without a hard ask | Berlin Community & Workshops | Medium-high — named specificity (Signature move #4) is what makes this work |
| **Timely trend response** | Relevance, authority | AI mainly, reactive not scheduled | Situational — only when there's something real to say, pulled from trend-analysis |

**Known from **voice.md**'s data**: how-to framing *without* a story wrapper underperforms (<50 likes). Every "practical tip" or "educational breakdown" post needs a real moment attached — never a standalone list of advice.

## 3. Sequencing & promotional guardrails

- **Workshop-mention cap: max 1 in every 3 posts** (from strategy). This includes announcements AND organic mentions — if a post exists mainly to remind people a workshop is happening, it counts.
- **Direct promotion vs. organic mention**: a direct promo asks for something (signups, comments). An organic mention (a workshop story, a participant quote) doesn't ask for anything and does NOT count toward engagement fatigue the same way — but still counts toward the 1-in-3 cap if workshops are the subject.
- **No format repeats back-to-back.** If last post was a personal story, the next shouldn't be too, regardless of pillar.
- **Around an actual workshop, sequence like this:**
  1. Value-led post (no mention of the workshop) — earns attention first
  2. Announcement (direct promo, "Comment [WORD]" CTA)
  3. Social proof / behind-the-scenes (organic, builds anticipation)
  4. Last-call reminder (direct, short, urgency from real scarcity — "keeping the group intentionally small," never fake)
  5. Post-event recap (organic, social proof for the *next* one)
- **Exception**: genuinely time-sensitive news (a major model release, something in her workshop space that's suddenly relevant) can jump the queue — but this is for real timeliness, not an excuse to skip planning by default.

## 4. How this connects to the rest of the system (no duplication)

- **content-pillars** → decides the topic. Posting-rhythm never overrides pillar mix, it operates alongside it — a week's plan has to satisfy both the pillar ratio and the format variety rule.
- **voice** → decides the tone/mode once pillar + format are picked. Posting-rhythm doesn't touch voice.
- **trend-analysis** (combined with the swipe file) → supplies the timely-response opportunities and the format/hook examples worth studying. Posting-rhythm consumes this, doesn't duplicate it.
- **strategy** → sets the business priorities (which workshop, which goal) that decide *when* the workshop sequence above gets triggered.
- **post-log** *(not yet built)* → will record what was actually posted, in what format, with what result. Posting-rhythm's evidence-led section (below) depends on this existing.

## 5. Weekly scheduling process

1. Gather approved ideas (from idea-capture, once it exists) and trend opportunities
2. Check current business priorities — any workshop/event this week or next?
3. Assign pillar to each idea (via content-pillars + pillar-mix-tracker check)
4. Assign format to each idea (via the table above) and voice mode (via voice.md)
5. Check: cadence for the week, format variety (no back-to-back repeats), workshop cap (1-in-3), sequencing if a workshop is active
6. Adjust before finalizing
7. After publishing, review results and feed into post-log — don't change the rhythm off one post, look for patterns over several weeks (same 2-3x repetition threshold used elsewhere in this system)

**Compact weekly checklist:**

- [ ] Pillar mix on track for last 10 posts?
- [ ] No format repeated from last post?
- [ ] Workshop mentions ≤ 1 in 3?
- [ ] At least one post this week pulled from LISTEN (trend-analysis or brain-dump)?
- [ ] Cadence matches the week type (normal / launch / quiet / news-driven)?

## 6. Making this evidence-led

Once post-log exists, evaluate **by format, separately from pillar**:

- Engagement and genuine comment conversation (not just likes)
- Workshop enquiries/signups traced to specific posts
- Signs of repetition fatigue (declining engagement on a format that used to work)

Rules carried from the rest of the system: a format only gets promoted from "test" to "priority" after repeating success **2-3 times**, not after one post. Competitor virality (from trend-analysis) is a reason to *test* a format, never proof it'll work here — that distinction stays intact.

**Status key for this file**: established rule / provisional assumption / active experiment — all marked inline above. Nothing here is being presented as proven except what already has real post data behind it (the "what lands vs. doesn't" figures from voice.md).
