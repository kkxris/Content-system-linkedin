# Ideas for posts

Write ideas here whenever they come to you — no need to develop or justify them first, that happens later. Claude reads this page directly when building content, so nothing needs to be copied or synced from anywhere else.

## How it's used

- You add ideas here directly, any time, any shape — half a sentence, a workshop moment, a reaction to something you saw
- When building a week's content, Claude reads everything in "New" below, cross-references it against `trend-analysis`, and runs it through `strategy` → `content-pillars` → `posting-rhythm`
- Once an idea's been picked up, Claude moves it to "Processed" with a one-line note on what happened to it — so "New" always shows only what hasn't been looked at yet
